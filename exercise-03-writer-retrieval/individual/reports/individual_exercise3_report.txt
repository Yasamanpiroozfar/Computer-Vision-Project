1. Starting point

I used the VLAD features produced by the group pipeline. The training file contains
1182 vectors and the test file contains 3600 vectors. Each VLAD vector has 12,800
dimensions.

The baseline result was:

Top-1 accuracy: 82.19%
mAP: 0.6297

Cosine similarity was used after L2 normalization. During evaluation, the query
image itself was removed from the ranked list.

2. Method 1: PCA with whitening

PCA was fitted only on the training VLAD vectors. The test vectors were then
transformed with the fitted PCA model, whitened, and L2-normalized.

Results:

PCA dimensions | Explained variance | Top-1 | mAP
128            | 0.7593             | 83.89% | 0.6668
256            | 0.8509             | 85.19% | 0.6952
512            | 0.9356             | 86.28% | 0.7105
1024           | 0.9940             | 86.94% | 0.7171

The best PCA result was obtained with 1024 dimensions. Compared with the baseline,
the mAP improved from 0.6297 to 0.7171.

3. Method 2: Reciprocal query expansion

For each query, I found its top-k nearest neighbours. A neighbour was used only
when the relationship was reciprocal, meaning that the neighbour also ranked the
query inside its own top-k list. The expanded query was the normalized sum of the
original query and the accepted reciprocal neighbours. Writer labels were not used
for this step.

Results on the original VLAD vectors:

k | Top-1 | mAP
1 | 82.19% | 0.6368
2 | 82.64% | 0.6463
3 | 82.75% | 0.6539
5 | 80.36% | 0.6471

The best result for this method was obtained with k=3. The mAP improved from
0.6297 to 0.6539. Using a larger neighbourhood reduced the result, probably
because less reliable neighbours were added.

4. Combination of both methods

I also applied reciprocal query expansion after PCA whitening with 1024
dimensions.

k | Top-1 | mAP
1 | 86.94% | 0.7268
2 | 87.06% | 0.7377
3 | 86.97% | 0.7460
4 | 86.78% | 0.7481
5 | 85.47% | 0.7408

The best mAP was 0.7481 with k=4. The best Top-1 accuracy was 87.06% with k=2.

5. Conclusion

Both individual methods improved the group baseline. PCA whitening gave the
largest improvement by itself. Reciprocal query expansion improved the ranking
further when it was applied after PCA. The final best result was:

Top-1 accuracy: 86.78%
mAP: 0.7481
